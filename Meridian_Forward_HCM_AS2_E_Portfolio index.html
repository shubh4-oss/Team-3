02 • HCM concepts

Translate strategy into roles and authority

The HR contribution is to connect business priorities to accountable work, realistic capacity, evidence-based selection and a clear governance model.

Strategic HR business partnering

SHRBP translates business priorities into role purpose, workforce options, performance measures and people processes. It recommends and coordinates; it does not automatically acquire commercial or technical approval authority.

Read 1 Mintzberg • Read 2 Dessler

Job analysis: JD vs JS

Job description (JD): outputs, duties, reporting, decision limits and escalation. Job specification (JS): knowledge, skills and capability evidence required to perform those duties.

D2 • Job design

Work design choices

Enlargement: add related tasks. Enrichment: add responsibility only where authority is genuinely delegated. Rotation: move across roles with training and service cover. Do not use job design to disguise an unresolved authority gap.

Why 216 h is not a net shortage

Total pilot demand is 1,868 h and potential supply is 1,984 h, so aggregate supply exceeds aggregate demand by 116 h. However, role-specific gaps total 216 h across data preparation, QA and legacy expertise.

Those figures can coexist because spare capacity in engineering, analytics or operations is not assumed to substitute for specialist skills. The decision must address the skills that are short, not only the aggregate hours.

Why this option?

  • Bounded workflow reduces the demand assigned to engineering, analytics and operations in the teaching scenario.
  • One authoritative customer-data output removes 240 h of duplicate preparation only if the receiving owner accepts the shared output.
  • It uses one data-preparation vendor plus one QA vendor and one legacy specialist in the baseline estimate.
  • Estimated vendor cost is ₹3.76 lakh against a ₹6.00 lakh illustrative budget, leaving ₹2.24 lakh headroom before unmodelled costs.

Defence questions

  1. Why can total potential supply exceed total demand while role-specific gaps still equal 216 h?
  2. Why does single-output preparation reduce demand only after receiving-owner acceptance?
  3. Why is the 600-position longer-term request not the same as four-week pilot staffing?
  4. Why is a selected worker not ready until access, cover and authority are cleared?
  5. Which values are teaching inputs, which are calculations, and which are student assumptions?
Limitations to state explicitlyThe workbook uses conditional full-release assumptions and baseline extra costs of zero where not supplied. Vendor start, proficiency, onboarding effort, service cover, permissions and approval are not all confirmed. Budget headroom is therefore not a guarantee of savings.
